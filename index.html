About us

Dera Karahi opened in 2015 with one wok, one tandoor and a simple idea: cook the way families cook at home, only with better ingredients and more patience.

Our karahis are made in small batches over a wood fire. Our naan is rolled and baked when you order it. Everything on the menu uses fresh, halal meat from trusted local suppliers.

Whether it’s a quick lunch, a family dinner or a late-night chai, there’s always a table for you.
